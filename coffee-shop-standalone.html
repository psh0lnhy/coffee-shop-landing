Story

We opened because the street needed a good flat white

In 2019 we took over a tiny kiosk with one grinder, two stools and a stubborn idea: coffee doesn't have to be complicated to be excellent. Six years later we roast in-house, bake before sunrise and still know most of our regulars by name.

No loyalty app, no QR menus at the table, no 12-step milk menu. Just good beans, clean equipment and baristas who care whether your shot pulled right.
